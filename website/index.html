The Gap

Two crises. One solution.

Thousands of work-ready refugees are locked out of opportunity while delivery platforms urgently need more riders. This is a structural gap — not a talent gap.

No Asset Access

Refugees cannot access motorcycle financing. Traditional lenders require credit history, payslips, or collateral — none of which this population has.

Digital Exclusion

Most eligible riders have never onboarded to Bolt, Uber, Glovo, or Faras. Without guidance, the verification process is a barrier they cannot cross alone.

Rising Fuel Costs

Petrol motorcycles face increasing operational costs eroding income. E-mobility offers a cleaner, cheaper alternative — but requires upfront financing.

Aid Dependency

Without structured pathways to ownership and income, capable individuals remain trapped in humanitarian aid that provides survival but no exit strategy.
