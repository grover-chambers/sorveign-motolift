Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on our platform.

Last updated: 27 May 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide information to the website owners. Cookies may be set by the website you visit ("first-party cookies") or by third parties such as analytics services ("third-party cookies").

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential/Strictly Necessary: required for the Platform to function (e.g., session management, security)
  • Functional: remember your preferences and settings (e.g., language, applicant type selection)
  • Analytics: understand how visitors interact with our Platform so we can improve it
  • Marketing: deliver relevant content and measure the effectiveness of our communications

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics: collects anonymised usage data to help us understand how visitors use our Platform. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: may collect usage data when loading fonts. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Social media platforms: if you use social sharing features, those platforms may set cookies. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Choices & Managing Cookies

You have the right to accept or reject cookies. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data

Please note that blocking essential cookies may impact the functionality of the Platform. You can also use your browser's incognito/private mode to limit cookie retention.

Cookie Consent: When you first visit our Platform, you will see a cookie notice. By continuing to browse, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings.

6. Consent Withdrawal

If you wish to withdraw your consent to cookies at any time, you can clear or block cookies using your browser settings (see section 5 above). Please note that withdrawing consent may affect your experience on our Platform.

7. Do Not Track

Our Platform does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. However, you can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings as described above.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
