FAQs

Common questions.

Quick answers to what riders ask us most.

How do I contact support? ▾
WhatsApp is fastest — reply within 30 minutes during business hours. Email within 4 hours. Phone support available Mon–Sat, 7am–7pm.
What languages is support available in? ▾
English, Swahili, French, Kinyarwanda, and Somali. Refugee applicants are matched with a community liaison who speaks their language.
What if I have a problem with my M-Pesa payments? ▾
Contact us via WhatsApp or phone. We can reverse failed deductions, correct overpayments, and adjust schedules within 24 hours.
My bike broke down — what do I do? ▾
Call our emergency hotline. We'll dispatch a mechanic or arrange a tow. If repairs take more than 48 hours, we provide a replacement bike.
Can I switch from one applicant type to another? ▾
Yes. If your status changes — for example, a refugee who later qualifies as a youth applicant — we update your profile and adjust your support accordingly.
Is there in-person support available? ▾
Yes. We have drop-in centres in Nairobi (CBD and Eastleigh) and regular outreach in Kakuma and Kalobeyei. Check our Contact Us page for addresses and hours.
