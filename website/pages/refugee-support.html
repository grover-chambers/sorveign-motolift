Eligible for Spiro and Ampersand e-motorcycle plans — lower daily costs, higher net income.

Build Credit History

M-Pesa repayments tracked through MotoLift — building formal financial history for future access.

Eligibility

You qualify if you are:

  • A UNHCR-registered refugee or asylum seeker in Kenya
  • Located in Nairobi, Kakuma, or surrounding urban areas
  • Aged 18 or above with a valid ID document
  • Willing to complete MotoLift rider training programme
