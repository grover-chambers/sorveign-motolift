Quick Answers

What documents do I need? ▾
Kenyan nationals need a national ID. Refugees need a UNHCR registration certificate. A driving licence is helpful but not required to apply.
How long does approval take? ▾
We respond within 24 hours. Full approval and onboarding is typically completed within 3–7 days, depending on document verification and platform registration.
Do I need a driving licence? ▾
Not to apply. We finance training for riders without a licence. You will need one before operating on delivery platforms, and we help you get it.
Is there an application fee? ▾
No. Applying is completely free. There are no upfront fees at any stage — financing is structured into your daily repayment plan.
No riding experience — can I still apply? ▾
Absolutely. Many of our riders start with zero experience. We provide basic training and pair you with experienced riders during your first weeks.
Can I change my bike later? ▾
Yes. The bike you select now is your preference. Final selection happens during onboarding once you've seen each bike and discussed options with our team.
What if I don't have a smartphone? ▾
A smartphone is needed for delivery apps and GPS navigation. We can help you access an affordable device through our device financing add-on. Let us know in your notes.
What languages are available? ▾
Our team and community liaisons support English, Swahili, French, Kinyarwanda, and Somali. We match you with a liaison who speaks your language.
