Our Core Partners

YVC and Jiwambe are at the heart of everything we do. They make refugee-led inclusion and accessible e-mobility financing possible. Everything else we build stands on this foundation.

Core Partners

Youth Voices Community (YVC)

Refugee-Led · Nairobi · Founded 2016

YVC builds awareness of refugee youth experiences while developing self-reliance through advocacy, education, and vocational training. Operating under the 3Ts — To Advocate, To Educate, To Empower — they reach 600+ beneficiaries annually via the REDUKA platform.

Mission

To advocate for and empower refugee youth by amplifying their voices, providing education, and fostering self-reliance through community-led initiatives.

Vision

A refugee-led society where every young person has the platform, resources, and dignity to shape their own future.

YVC's community credibility is the bridge that makes our outreach meaningful. They recruit, vet, and mentor riders from within the communities we serve — ensuring inclusion is led by those who understand it best.

Jiwambe

E-Mobility Financing · Nairobi · Daily Payments

Jiwambe provides accessible credit for e-motorbikes and business tools with flexible daily payments. With 50+ assets financed, they require only ID, income proof, and two guarantors — lowering the barrier for riders who traditional lenders turn away.

Mission

To make e-mobility financing accessible to underserved communities by replacing upfront lump sums with pay-as-you-earn daily installments.

Vision

A Nairobi where every motivated individual — regardless of background — can access clean, sustainable transportation and build a livelihood around it.

Jiwambe's flexible financing model aligns perfectly with our refugee-first approach — enabling riders to access e-motorbikes with minimal upfront costs and pay-as-you-earn daily installments.

Delivery Platform Partners
Delivery platforms are where our riders earn immediately from day one. By partnering with Bolt, Glovo, Uber, and Faras, we ensure new riders have instant access to paying work — no waiting periods, no slow starts. These platforms provide the demand; we provide the riders and the bikes.

Bolt

Kenya's largest ride-hailing and food delivery platform with the highest order volume in Nairobi. Our primary onboarding corridor for new riders.

Riders earn immediately on Bolt's network from day one of financing. Flexible hours, daily cash-outs, and a massive customer base mean consistent income.

Glovo

Fast-growing last-mile delivery platform across Nairobi with shorter delivery routes ideal for new riders building confidence.

Glovo's delivery-focus means predictable routes and tips. Preferred launch platform for riders with zero delivery experience.

Uber / Uber Eats

Global ride-hailing and delivery giant operating in Kenya. Access to both passenger and delivery markets from a single account.

Dual earning streams — riders can switch between Uber rides and Uber Eats deliveries. Global brand recognition opens future mobility opportunities.

Faras

Kenya-based digital transport platform with deep local market knowledge and flexible rider onboarding requirements.

Reduces dependency on international platforms. Faras' local-first approach means faster support and Kenyan-market-specific earnings optimisations.

E-Mobility Partners
Our fleet is 100% electric. Spiro, Cheche, Tankvolt, and Enzi supply the bikes that keep our riders moving — with swappable batteries, extended range, and locally-built reliability designed for African roads.

Spiro

Electric motorcycle manufacturer with the largest battery-swap network in Kenya. Zero charging downtime keeps riders earning.

Swappable batteries eliminate range anxiety. Lowest daily financing rate in our fleet — ideal for entry-level riders maximising take-home earnings.

Cheche

Kenya-built electric motorcycle with extended range and rapid charging. Designed specifically for African roads and urban delivery conditions.

Extended range means fewer charging stops during peak delivery hours. Local manufacturing ensures faster spare parts availability and lower maintenance costs.

Tankvolt

High-performance electric motorcycle with 2,200-cycle battery life and 88 km/h top speed. Built for serious daily delivery volumes.

Exceptional battery longevity means lower long-term cost. Higher speed capability opens longer-distance delivery routes with premium earnings.

Enzi

Premium Kenyan e-motorcycle brand with robust build quality and long-range capability. The aspirational upgrade in our fleet.

Maximum daily earning potential through longer range and premium positioning. Natural upgrade path for riders who have built credit and want more.
