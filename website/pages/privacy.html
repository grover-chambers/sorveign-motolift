2. What Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through your use of our platform:

  • Identity data: full name, date of birth, nationality, refugee/ asylum-seeker documentation (where applicable), national ID or alien ID number
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, physical address
  • Financial data: M-Pesa account details, transaction history, repayment records
  • Applicant data: applicant type (Refugee / Youth / YVC), YVC reference code, employment status, riding experience
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on our platform
  • Communication data: correspondence with our support team, including WhatsApp messages and call recordings (where applicable)

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR and Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019), we process your data on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: where you have given clear consent for us to process your data for a specific purpose (e.g., marketing communications)
  • Contract performance: processing necessary to enter into or perform our financing agreement with you
  • Legal obligation: processing required to comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Legitimate interests: processing necessary for our legitimate business interests (e.g., fraud prevention, service improvement), where such interests do not override your fundamental rights

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Processing your financing application and assessing eligibility
  • Managing your financing agreement, including M-Pesa repayments
  • Communicating with you about your account, payments, and support requests
  • Facilitating onboarding with delivery platforms (Bolt, Glovo, Uber Eats, Faras)
  • Improving our platform, services, and user experience
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Sending service updates and (with your consent) promotional communications

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and for three years after your last interaction with our platform, or as required by applicable law. After this period, your data is securely deleted or anonymised.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and Kenya's Data Protection Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten): request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to restriction: request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability: request transfer of your data to another service provider
  • Right to object: object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent: withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint: lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya) or your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at crescentkasuki@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your data only where necessary and with appropriate safeguards:

  • Delivery platforms: Bolt, Glovo, Uber Eats, and Faras — for account onboarding and verification
  • Payment processors: M-Pesa (Safaricom) — for loan disbursement and repayment processing
  • Community partners: YVC Kenya — for applicant verification and progress reporting (with your consent)
  • Service providers: hosting, analytics, and communication platforms that process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements
  • Regulatory authorities: where required by law or to protect our legal rights

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

8. International Data Transfers

Where we transfer your data to third parties outside Kenya, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms recognised under the GDPR and Kenya's Data Protection Act.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff training on data protection
  • Incident response procedures
