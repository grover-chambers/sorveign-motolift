About SOrvReign MotoLift

Mobility to Income.

SOrvReign MotoLift connects refugees and underserved youth in Kenya to e-mobility financing, delivery platform employment, and long-term asset ownership. We do not simply finance motorcycles. We finance livelihoods.

"What if we could connect people who desperately need opportunity with an industry that urgently needs workers?" — That question became the foundation of SOrvReign MotoLift. Born from observing thousands of capable, willing people excluded from economic opportunity that is within reach — simply because systems have not been built to include them.

The Gap & Our Response

Two crises. One solution.

Thousands of work-ready people are locked out of opportunity while delivery platforms urgently need more riders. This is a structural gap — not a talent gap.

⚠ The Crisis
🔒

No Asset Access

Refugees cannot access motorcycle financing. Traditional lenders require credit history, payslips, or collateral — none of which this population has.

💻

Digital Exclusion

Most eligible riders have never onboarded to Bolt, Uber, Glovo, or Faras. The app setup and verification process is a barrier they cannot cross alone.

📋

Documentation Barriers

Even refugees with valid UNHCR asylum documentation face bureaucratic hurdles accessing employment platforms and financial services.

⛽

Rising Fuel Costs

Petrol motorcycle riders face increasing operational costs eroding income. No structured transition exists for affordable electric alternatives.

🔗

Aid Dependency Cycle

Without structured pathways to ownership and income, capable individuals remain trapped in humanitarian aid that provides survival but no exit strategy.

➔
✔ The MotoLift Response
1

Motorcycle Asset Financing

Structured installment plans with repayment calibrated to projected daily earnings. Both petrol and electric options available.

2

Digital Platform Onboarding

End-to-end registration on Bolt, Uber, Glovo, and Faras — including documentation support, app training, account verification, and first-ride guidance.

3

Multilingual Rider Training

Financial literacy, road safety, customer service, and digital skills training in Swahili, French, and English — accessible to Congolese, South Sudanese, and Rwandan refugees.

4

Repayment Management & Credit Building

M-Pesa integrated repayment tracking, rider dashboards, and analytics. Consistent repayment unlocks insurance, savings, and microcredit products.

5

Electric Mobility Transition

Partnerships with e-motorcycle providers (Spiro, Ampersand) reduce fuel costs by up to 60%, increase net rider income, and position the platform as climate-positive.

6

Refugee Community Support

Purpose-built refugee onboarding with UNHCR coordination, RAS documentation guidance, and multilingual support through trusted community liaisons from DRC, South Sudan, and Rwanda.

The Model

Income first. Repayment follows.

Every step in our model generates value for the rider, making repayment a natural outcome of economic success — not a burden imposed from above.

Access Financing

Rider receives a motorcycle through a structured installment plan calibrated to projected daily earnings. No credit history required.

Platform Onboarding

We handle Bolt, Glovo, Uber, or Faras registration. Rider is verified, trained, and earning within 7 days of financing.

Generate Income

Rider earns daily through deliveries and rides. Dashboard tracks earnings vs. repayment schedule in real time.

Repay & Build Credit

M-Pesa repayments build formal financial history. Consistent repayment unlocks insurance, savings, and microcredit products.

Own the Asset

Motorcycle fully owned after repayment. Rider becomes independent operator — eligible to mentor new riders or access fleet expansion.
